First-Time Home Buyers

+

Buying your first home can feel overwhelming. You may have questions about credit, down payments, closing costs, inspections, loan programs, neighborhoods, and how much you should actually spend.

You do not have to figure it out alone. I'll help you understand the process from preparing your finances through closing day.

First-Time Buyer Checklist

  • Determine your comfortable monthly payment
  • Review income, employment, and credit
  • Connect with an appropriate mortgage professional
  • Learn about down-payment assistance programs
  • Review available loan options
  • Establish your estimated purchase budget
  • Discuss neighborhoods and lifestyle priorities
  • Begin your home search
  • Write and negotiate an offer
  • Schedule inspections
  • Review appraisal and financing milestones
  • Prepare for closing
  • Complete the final walkthrough
  • Close and receive your keys

I Can Help You With

  • Understanding the home-buying process
  • Connecting you with mortgage professionals
  • Finding properties within your goals
  • Comparing neighborhoods
  • Offer strategy and negotiations
  • Inspection coordination
  • Contract-to-close guidance
  • Connecting you with trusted real estate professionals

Your first home doesn't have to be perfect. It needs to be the right next step.

Veterans & Military Home Buyers

Your Service Matters. Your Home Should Too.
+

Veterans and active-duty military families may have access to valuable home-buying benefits, including VA financing options. I understand that buying a home can come with a different set of questions for military families, especially when it comes to VA eligibility, financing, occupancy requirements, inspections, timelines, PCS moves, and relocation.

My role is to help make the process easier to understand and connect you with professionals who can help you navigate the details.

VA Buyer Checklist

  • Obtain your Certificate of Eligibility or confirm eligibility with your lender
  • Connect with a VA-experienced mortgage professional
  • Review your income, debts, and overall budget
  • Determine your comfortable monthly payment
  • Discuss VA loan benefits and requirements
  • Identify preferred locations and commute requirements
  • Search for eligible properties
  • Submit and negotiate an offer
  • Complete required inspections and due diligence
  • Coordinate appraisal and lender requirements
  • Review closing documents
  • Complete your final walkthrough
  • Close on your home

I Can Help With

  • Connecting you with VA-experienced lending professionals
  • Property searches
  • Relocation assistance
  • Offer strategy
  • Negotiations
  • Inspection coordination
  • Contract guidance
  • Local market education
  • Connecting military families with trusted service providers

Thank you for serving. Let's make your home-buying experience feel supported, informed, and organized.

Move-Up Buyers

When Your First Home No Longer Fits Your Life
+

Maybe your family has grown. Maybe you are working from home. Maybe you're ready for more space, a different neighborhood, a better school district, or simply a home that fits where you are today.

Moving up can involve selling one home while purchasing another, which requires careful timing.

Move-Up Buyer Checklist

  • Determine your new housing goals
  • Review your current mortgage and equity position
  • Estimate your home's current market value
  • Determine your purchase budget
  • Discuss whether to sell first or buy first
  • Review available financing
  • Identify target neighborhoods
  • Prepare your existing home for sale
  • Begin the purchase search
  • Coordinate the sale and purchase timelines
  • Negotiate offers
  • Manage inspections, appraisal, and financing
  • Coordinate closing dates
  • Transition into your next home

My goal is to help you create a plan that considers both sides of the move, rather than treating the purchase and sale as two completely separate transactions.

Downsizing Checklist

  • Research environmental concerns
  • Verify easements and restrictions
  • Review topography
  • Research nearby development
  • Review taxes
  • Investigate financing options
  • Complete appropriate due diligence

I can help you identify properties and connect you with the professionals needed to investigate whether a piece of land fits your intended use.

My Story

Before I Was a REALTOR®,
Real Estate Was Already Part of My Life.

My connection to real estate started when I was just 18 years old. My mother owned her own mortgage company, and I grew up around the language, paperwork, people, decisions, and responsibilities that come with helping someone finance a home. Eventually, I became a broker for the mortgage company.

That experience gave me a perspective that I carry with me today: real estate is never just about the property. It is about the person behind the transaction.

The Long Way Around

Over the years, I have worked with several mortgage companies and gained experience in areas including underwriting, closing documentation, and loan-related processes. I have also worked as a Notary Signing Agent.

Those experiences taught me how important the details are.

A missed document can delay a closing.

A misunderstood number can affect a budget.

A rushed decision can create problems long after closing.

I learned to pay attention. I learned to ask questions. I learned that people don't just need someone to open doors. They need someone who can help them understand what is happening behind those doors.

Today, I am a licensed Georgia REALTOR® with Premier Agent Network, and I am expanding my real estate service into Florida as well.

But the title is not the part of this work that matters most to me.
